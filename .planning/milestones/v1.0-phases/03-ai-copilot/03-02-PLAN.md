<objective>
Make the assistant act, honestly. Trades and watchlist changes it names in its structured reply
auto-execute through the exact code paths the manual trade bar and watchlist panel use, and the
result — filled or rejected — comes back inline as a confirmation card built from what actually
happened rather than from what the model asked for.

**User story (MVP):** **As a** FinAlly trader, **I want to** tell the assistant to buy, sell, or
change my watchlist in plain language, **so that** it executes for me and tells me truthfully
whether it worked.

Purpose: this is the phase's highest-stakes invariant. Deriving the reported actions from
executor return values is what makes "claimed a trade that never happened" structurally
impossible in the machine-readable half of the response (AI-SPEC EV-1, CFM #1).

Output: `app/llm/executor.py`, the full D-11 mock rule table plus the 12-scenario reference
dataset that backs it, and the inline `TradeConfirmationCard` in its success and REJECTED
variants.
</objective>
