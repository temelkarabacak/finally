<objective>
Make the copilot durable and honest when things go wrong, and welcoming when the conversation is
empty. The transcript survives a reload, a hung or broken model call degrades to one shared,
plainly generic message with nothing executed and nothing corrupted, and a first-time user gets
three one-click prompts instead of a blank box.

**User story (MVP):** **As a** FinAlly trader, **I want to** come back to my conversation after a
reload, get an obvious and harmless failure when the assistant cannot answer, and have something
to click when I do not know what to ask, **so that** the copilot feels trustworthy rather than
brittle.

Purpose: this closes the phase's two remaining critical failure modes — a malformed response
crashing or half-executing a turn (CFM #2), and the timeout path executing a trade or leaving a
partial `chat_messages` row (CFM #4) — and completes the UI-SPEC's loading, empty, and error
state contract.

Output: `GET /api/chat/history`, the TEST-02 degradation suite, the CHAT-05 transaction-split
suite, a manual live smoke script, and the drawer's quick-prompt, loading, and error states.
</objective>

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
